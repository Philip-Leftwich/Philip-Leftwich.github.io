Research, teaching, and data science for modern genetics.

Hi, I’m Phil!

I am an Associate Professor in Genetics & Data Science at the University of East Anglia. My work spans insect genetics, microbiomes, and quantitative biology, alongside teaching in molecular biology, genetics, statistics, and computing skills.

Research at a glance

My research focuses on applied genetics, host-microbe interactions, and the tools needed to study and teach modern biology.

Genetic pest management

I work on gene drives, transgene design, and molecular tools for agricultural pests and disease vectors.

Microbiomes and evolution

I study how gut microbes influence physiology, reproduction, and evolutionary dynamics in insect systems.
